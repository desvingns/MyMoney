Keep a clear record of everyday money with MyMoney.

MyMoney is a personal expense tracker for recording expenses, income, and transfers between your accounts. Add a record with an amount, category, date, account, currency, and note, then see your balance and spending at a glance.

What you can do:
• Track expenses and income in seconds.
• Record transfers between accounts, including transfers between different currencies with an exchange rate.
• Use day, week, month, year, all-time, or custom date-range views.
• See spending by category in a donut chart, follow balance trends, and open the records behind a summary.
• Search, filter, edit, and delete transactions. Undo a deletion during the available undo period.
• Create and reorder categories, choose icons and colors, and manage accounts and currencies.
• Set category budgets and see an in-app warning when spending goes over a budget.
• Add recurring transactions for daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly entries.
• Create savings goals and see calculated progress and dates.
• Choose light, dark, or system theme. Use English or Russian. Turn sound and haptic feedback on or off.
• Protect the app with biometric unlock and a fallback PIN when supported by your device.
• Export or import a full database backup (.db) through the Android file picker.
• Export transactions to CSV and import CSV records through the guided import flow.

Storage and control:
Your entries are stored in the app's local database on your device. You can choose a folder for database backups or a file for CSV export and import using Android's file picker. The standard build does not enable cloud synchronization by default. Optional network integrations, if available in a particular build, require their own account or configuration.

MyMoney is a tracking tool. It does not connect to banks or provide banking, investment, lending, credit, payment, or other financial services.
