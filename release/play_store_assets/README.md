# Play Store Assets

Product owns the approved artwork and listing copy for the first Play Console
submission. Review the final screenshots and copy in Play Console before
publishing the public release.

## Screenshots

Provide eight phone screenshots in the required Play Console dimensions:

1. `01_dashboard_day.png` - dashboard overview and donut chart.
2. `02_add_expense.png` - expense entry flow.
3. `03_add_income.png` - income entry flow.
4. `04_transactions.png` - transactions history and search.
5. `05_budgets.png` - budget visibility or warning state.
6. `06_accounts.png` - accounts and currencies management.
7. `07_backup_sync.png` - backup or gated cloud-sync screen.
8. `08_settings_theme.png` - settings, theme, or language selection.

## Graphics

- `feature_graphic.png` - approved Play Store feature graphic.
- Adaptive app icon export - approved foreground/background artwork and store icon.

## Listing Copy

- `short_description_en.txt` / `short_description_ru.txt` - short descriptions,
  maximum 80 characters each.
- `full_description_en.txt` / `full_description_ru.txt` - full descriptions,
  maximum 4000 characters each.
