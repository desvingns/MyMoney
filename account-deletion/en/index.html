MyMoney account and data deletion

Developer: Kirill Shavrin · Last updated: August 11, 2026

Important implementation note

The production Supabase migration for the authenticated deletion function delete_my_account has been deployed. The release build includes an authenticated in-app deletion path. It is blocked while the user has an active Shared workspace with other members; resolve that membership first. If the app is inaccessible, use this public page or the email request process; requests are reviewed manually by the developer.

The release build includes optional Shared workspaces. Users can sign in with Google OAuth through Supabase Auth. Dropbox and Google Drive copies are controlled by the user's own provider accounts and are not removed automatically by account deletion.

Request account and data deletion

Or email desvingns@gmail.com with the subject MyMoney account deletion request.

How to request deletion

  1. Use the button above or send an email to desvingns@gmail.com.
  2. Include the email address of the Google account used with MyMoney.
  3. If relevant, include the workspace identifier. Do not include a password, OAuth token, access token, refresh token, or financial records.
  4. We may ask for reasonable information needed to verify the request before processing it.

What the deletion process can remove

  • Supabase Auth profile/user: the authenticated in-app path deletes the user's Supabase Auth user and related account data. A sole-owner workspace is deleted when allowed; a shared workspace with other members is not deleted.
  • Workspace membership: an editor can leave; a sole owner can delete the workspace.
  • Active Shared workspaces with other members: account deletion is blocked safely until the membership is resolved; it does not delete a shared workspace or other members' data.
  • Invitations: workspace deletion cascades to workspace invites.
  • Operations and related records: workspace deletion cascades to operations, conflicts, members and other workspace-linked records.
  • Session and tokens: the deletion flow clears the locally stored Supabase access/refresh tokens and session binding.
  • Dropbox and Google Drive copies: files already stored in the user's provider account remain there and require provider-side deletion.
  • Local financial data: deleting the remote account does not erase the local Room database; use the app's factory reset, clear storage, or uninstall the app.

The workspace-deletion operation is owner-only and is restricted to a sole owner. If the account has an active workspace with other members, leave it first; the deletion operation will not remove other members or their shared data.

Data on your device

Signing out or disconnecting Shared workspaces clears the local workspace binding, synchronization state, outbox and local Supabase session. It does not by itself erase the Room database containing local financial data. To remove local data, use the app's factory-reset controls, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Android backups, exported files and copies made by the user may require separate deletion.

Google Drive and Dropbox copies

Disconnecting Google Drive or Dropbox removes the local credential and synchronization binding, but the current app does not automatically delete already uploaded backup or journal files in those provider accounts. Remote copies still require provider-side deletion: delete them directly in Google Drive or Dropbox, and mention any known copy or account details in your request if you need help identifying them.

Processing time and legal retention

We aim to review a request and reply within 30 days. Requests may take longer if ownership must be verified or a provider-side action is required; we will explain the reason.

Information may be retained only when required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. Any retained information is limited to what is necessary and is deleted when the legal reason ends.
